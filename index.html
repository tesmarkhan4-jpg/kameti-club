Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Welcome to Kameti Club. Please read these Terms and Conditions ("T&C") carefully before registering. By checking the agreement checkbox during sign-up, you legally bind yourself to these terms under the Electronic Transactions Ordinance (ETO), 2002 of Pakistan.

1. Nature of Business (Kameti Savings)

Kameti Club operates strictly as a digital ledger, verification coordinator, and communications platform for Rotating Savings (commonly known as "Kameti" or "Kameti" in Pakistan). By joining a group, you agree to form a 10-member peer savings circle rotating over 10 consecutive months.

2. Financial Commitments & Fees

  • Base Deposit: Each member must pay their group's base kameti amount (ranging from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 20,000 depending on group capital) on a monthly basis.
  • Admin Service Fee: Each member must pay a flat Rs. 100 per month in addition to the base deposit. This fee is used for platform operations, identity verification, SMS notifications, and support overhead.
  • Payout Rotation: Every member is entitled to receive the total pooled kameti funds (excluding admin fees) exactly **one time** during the 10-month cycle.

3. Deadline, Late Fee Penalties & Law Actions

  • Due Date: Payments must be verified and logged in the app on or before the 10th date of each calendar month.
  • Late Fee Penalty: Any member who fails to make payments before the 10th of the month will be charged a strict overcharge penalty of Rs. 500 per day for each day the payment is delayed.
  • Defaulter & Legal Recovery Consent: By agreeing to these terms, you grant explicit consent that in the event of failure to pay within 30 days of the due date, the administrator and members of the group have the right to register a criminal complaint (under sections 406/420 PPC for criminal breach of trust and fraud) and seek recovery through summary court procedures. Your uploaded CNIC Front & Back biometrics and address details will be submitted to the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) and local law enforcement for prosecution.

4. Urgent Payout Swap Rules

Members can submit request forms for urgent next-month payouts. These requests will only be approved if the member scheduled for the next month agrees to swap cycles, and if the admin approves the exchange based on membership performance history.

5. Peer-to-Peer Transactions Disclaimer

Kameti Club itself does not hold or store digital money or wallet balances on its servers. All payments are manually transferred directly to the designated Admin banking coordinates and audited via the system ledgers. The operator is not responsible for external bank downtime or transfer failures.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

At Kameti Club, we take user privacy and identity security very seriously. This policy describes how we collect, store, and utilize your information under the digital privacy guidelines of Pakistan.

1. Information We Collect

To participate in our secure kametis, users must provide:

  • Full Name, Registered Email Address, and Active WhatsApp Number.
  • High-definition photographs of your CNIC Front and Back sides for identity verification and anti-fraud auditing.
  • Account Details: EasyPaisa, JazzCash, NayaPay, SadaPay, or Bank account numbers and Account Title for payout processing.

2. Data Security & Storage

Your uploaded CNIC images, contact details, and account keys are converted into encrypted data URLs and stored securely. We do not sell or share your biometrics or credentials with any commercial third-party marketing networks.

3. Law Enforcement Disclosures

We are legally required to disclose CNIC documents, registered emails, IP logs, and payment details to the Government of Pakistan, Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), State Bank of Pakistan (SBP), or the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) only when requested during investigations of default, financial fraud, money laundering, or illegal asset tracking.

4. User Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account once all your joined kameti cycles are completed and all financial liabilities have been fully paid. Active kameti participants cannot request profile deletion during a running cycle.
